# Code of conduct

exposehub should be a project people of any background and any level of experience can take part in without being harassed or talked down to. This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas and code, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when something does not work for you.
- Give and take review comments as help. Explain your reasons; ask when a comment is unclear.
- Remember that maintainers and contributors give their own time.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, and derogatory comments about anyone's gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, ethnicity, religion, age or experience
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Harassment in public or in private, including continuing to contact someone who asked you to stop
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or employer, without their consent

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer, Jakob Bennemann, in private: through the contact details on the GitHub profile [github.com/jkbennemann](https://github.com/jkbennemann). Say what happened, where, and add links or screenshots if you have them. Reports are kept confidential, and you get an answer within a week.

Security problems are a different matter and go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What happens then

The maintainer reads every report and decides what fits what happened: a private word, a public correction, removing a comment, a temporary ban, or a permanent one for repeated or serious cases. Comments, commits and issues that break these rules may be edited or removed.

For a fuller statement of the same intent, see the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which this text follows in spirit.
