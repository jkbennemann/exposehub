# Legal texts

Put the operator's imprint and privacy notice here as Markdown:

- `imprint.md` is published at `/imprint`
- `privacy.md` is published at `/privacy`

The pages and the links to them exist only while the files do, and a
change shows on the next page load.

Starting points for both are in `web/resources/legal/` of this repository
(`imprint.example.md`, `privacy.example.md`). They are drafts, not legal
advice: fill in every `[...]` and have them checked for your situation.
`scripts/backup.sh` includes this directory.
