## Liability for content carried through tunnels

machines. The content at a tunnel address comes from the user who opened the
tunnel, not from the provider. Under §§ 7 to 10 DDG and Art. 4 to 6 of the
Digital Services Act the provider is not obliged to monitor transmitted
information, and acts on unlawful content once it has knowledge of it.
